## Defects found by real smoke

The first direct real call proved `mlx-whisper` raises `NotImplementedError` when `beam_size` is set;
its source explicitly says the beam decoder is not implemented. LiveClip removed that unsupported
option and advanced the immutable ASR contract to v2. Temperature-zero greedy decoding remains fixed.

The first adapter smoke returned an empty transcript for known non-silent audio. Independent
`volumedetect` measured approximately -24.4 dB mean and -1.7 dB maximum. Root cause: M4A/MP4 needs
seekable input, but the adapter gave FFmpeg `pipe:0`; FFmpeg emitted only a header and still returned
success. The repaired adapter passes the already pinned and SHA-verified regular-file descriptor as
`/dev/fd/N`, inherits that exact descriptor with `shell=False`, and rejects decoded PCM shorter than
75% of the declared chunk duration.

“可以大在公屏上” and “摸镜练买”; human-labelled audio is required before either transcript can be
scored as correct.
