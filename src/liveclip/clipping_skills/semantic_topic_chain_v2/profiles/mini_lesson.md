is a target, not padding permission. Related definition, explanation, demonstration,
practice, steps and conclusion may belong together. Choose the shortest complete
boundaries. No jump cuts, mechanical 300-second chunks or duration-based semantic merges.

evidence, or topic_too_long_without_natural_split rejection.
