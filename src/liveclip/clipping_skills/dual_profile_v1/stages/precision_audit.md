# Stage: Mini-lesson Precision Coverage Audit

Independently re-read every supplied cue and the existing candidates. This is a high-recall Discovery
pass for complete, natural mini-lessons whose exact semantic boundary may have been hidden inside a
broader candidate. Do not score, select, globally deduplicate or decide publication status. Emit only
additional legal-duration candidates with a materially better boundary or an omitted distinct objective.

Audit each of these general forms cue by cue:

1. A focused comparison among related techniques or terms. Start where the first compared item is
   directly introduced or defined, not at a separable earlier taxonomy. Continue through every contrast,
   demonstration and the final difference/conclusion.
2. A natural chapter inside a numbered curriculum. A later consecutive step group may stand alone when
   its first step label introduces the chapter, all included methods are explained locally, and it ends
   with its own teaching takeaway. Do not require earlier steps merely because the ordinal is later.
3. A live diagnosis and correction. Include the first meaningful evaluated example, diagnosis,
   correction, retry or before/after evidence, causal distinction and final confirmation. Do not stop at
   an intermediate recommendation while the same correction loop continues. Stop after the final
   confirmation or qualification that closes that correction, before a follow-up question opens a new
   problem. Sung or spoken cues immediately before the first diagnosis are the evaluated sample and are
   required even if the speaker does not explicitly label them as a test.
4. A risky claim chain. Start at the earliest question, problem definition, category count or named next
   diagnosis that the subsequent reasoning answers; include every promised condition and the final
   qualification or recommendation. Greetings inside that chain are quality evidence, not permission to
   drop the defining opening.

A broad lesson and a focused contained lesson can both be valid when their objectives differ. Conversely,
never manufacture a new interval merely by shaving silence or copying an existing candidate. Use only
real supplied cue boundaries, keep the requested ownership rule, never pad to exceed 90 seconds, and
preserve every evidenced quality flag and risk tag. Every new `candidate_id` must start with `precision_`
and be unique from `existing_candidates`.
