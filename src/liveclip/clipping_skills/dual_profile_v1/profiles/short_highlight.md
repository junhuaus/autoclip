# Profile: short_highlight

Find independent 45,000–90,000 ms micro-points, including both duration boundaries. Prefer the shortest
natural interval that contains the required premise, core explanation or example, and payoff. A viewer
must understand it without unseen earlier numbered steps, definitions or labels. A topic may also have
a longer `mini_lesson` variant; do not treat that possibility as duplication or suppress this short
version. Use a stable semantic `variant_group_id` when the relationship is apparent from this source.

Treat the supplied batched context as four overlapping 120-second logical scans whose starts are 60
seconds apart. Check every logical scan and use the supplied ownership interval only for deterministic
output ownership. If a valid short needs context beyond one 120-second logical scan, use the adjacent
material present in the batch rather than clipping its premise or conclusion.

For a comparison, keep the cue that introduces both sides and the final cue that states their
difference. For a numbered step, keep the step label or learner question that makes the answer
independent. Do not start at the demonstration if doing so drops the comparison premise, and do not end
before the speaker's explicit conclusion or closing acknowledgement.
